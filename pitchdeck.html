10%–15% Stratified Random Sampling Methodology

Operational efficiency for multi-thousand hectare reforestation based on certified forestry statistics.
Why Adopt Random Sampling?
• Auditing 100,000 trees individually every month is cost-prohibitive.
• Adopts internationally recognized Stratified Random Sampling standards.
• Divides plantation terrain into uniform 1–2 hectare blocks.
• Audits 10%–15% randomly designated trees per block on monthly inspection cycles.
Statistical Confidence & Rigor
• Confidence Level: ≥95%.
• Margin of Error: Under ±3.5%.
• Field Operational Savings: Reduces routine monitoring overhead by 85%.
• ESG Compliance: Formally recognized by independent sustainability auditing firms.
